How it goes

  1. Today About 15 minutes. A few tiny pinches. No downtime.
  2. 1 week Most people see the change.
  3. 2 weeks The full effect.
  4. 3 to 4 months It wears off. Time to come back.

Typical times, not promises. Results vary from person to person.

Is it right for me?

Tell Aaron every medicine you take. Wait if you:

  • are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • have an infection where we would treat
  • have certain nerve or muscle conditions

Side effects

  • Most people: a small red spot or a light bruise. It fades in a few days.
  • Some people: a mild headache for a day.
  • Rarely: an eyelid or brow feels heavy for a few weeks.

Aaron goes over all of this with you before you decide.
