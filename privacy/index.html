Privacy

Last updated: November 2026.

This page says what we collect, why, and what we never do.

What we collect

Only what you give us: your name, phone number, e-mail address and your message when you call, text or e-mail us. At a visit, Aaron keeps your medical record in the practice's patient system, not on this website.

If you fill in our patient form, we also get your date of birth. The form runs on Google Forms, in the practice's own account.

How we use it

  • To answer you and to book your visit.
  • To send you reminders about your treatment, by e-mail or text, if you asked for them.
  • To tell you about our services, by e-mail, until you tell us to stop.

To stop reminders or news, reply STOP to a text, click the unsubscribe link in an e-mail, or e-mail care@miamimedicalaesthetics.com. We take you off the list within ten business days.

What we never do

  • We never sell your information.
  • We never share it with advertisers.
  • This website sets no tracking cookies.

How we protect it

Your information is kept in password-protected accounts that only the practice can open. If we ever learn that it was exposed, we tell you as Florida law requires.

Your choices

You can ask what we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your contact information. E-mail care@miamimedicalaesthetics.com.

Who we are
